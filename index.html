Professional Impact

01

Sales Development Territory Split

The Challenge: 70% of inbound leads from "Greenfield" accounts forced Territory ADRs to spend significant time on low-probability inbound inquiries rather than generating sales-ready pipeline within strategic accounts.
The Solution:
  • Created specialized Inbound ADR team for high-volume unowned segment
  • Prioritized paired ADRs efforts to focus exclusively on named accounts
  • Drive deeper territory alignment with paired AEs
Impact Optimized workflow by aligning resources with lead sources, delivering higher-quality opportunities to AEs
02

ADR Onboarding Improvements

The Challenge: High velocity of ADR hiring led to inconsistent onboarding experiences, resulting in varied readiness levels and prolonged ramp times.
Outcomes Focused Progression:
  • Week 1-2: Standardized peer shadowing goals and requirements.
  • Week 3: Sandbox on recycled leads for low-risk skill development
  • Week 4: Developed thorough onboarding curriculum and assessment rubric
Impact Reduced ramp time by 20% while maintaining pipeline quality through standardized and hands-on onboarding
03

Territory & Packaging Strategy Shift

The Context:New fiscal brought dramatic territory shift to SMB segment (<250 employees) with new add-ons threatening to cannibalize Enterprise revenue within segment.
Key Insights:
  • Closed Opportunity Analysis: Facilitated deep analysis on Closed Won/Lost opportunities from within the segment to identify trends
  • Territory Prioritization Plays: Revamped territory prioritization plays to focus efforts materially on high-value accounts while maintaining low-effort points across full book
  • AE Enablement and Message House: Coordinated AE enablement efforts to ensure alignment with new territory and packaging strategies.
Impact Shifted from usage-based selling to value-based selling, protecting Enterprise revenue while expanding footprint in SMB segment
04

Messaging Performance Optimization

The Insight: Analysis of outbound messaging debunked "more volume = more results" assumption.
Data-Driven Changes:
  • Analyzed meetings booked against various sequences, normalized by volume
  • Identified stronger performance with manually personalized messaging vs. automated sequences
  • Implemented Trigger-Based sequences over generic vertical messaging
Impact Mandated "80% effort above Power Line" with high-effort personalization on high-value personas, driving increased meeting conversion rates
05

Trigger-Based Selling Operations

The Challenge: 1,169 activity and behavior alerts sent to reps over 30 days showed 37.55% were low-intent noise while high-value signals received generic follow-ups.
Signal-to-Play Matrix:
  • Reducing Noise: Eliminated low-intent signals, focusing enablement and sequences on high-value triggers
  • Messaging: Implemented targeted follow-ups for high-value triggers utilizing data-driven insights on top performing sequences
  • Standardization: Established clear protocols for signal management and follow-up execution
Impact Deprecated dead signals, reduced rep fatigue, shifted focus to high-intent behaviors, improving engagement rates
06

Custom AI Support GPT

The Challenge: ADR team faced knowledge fragmentation across disconnected resources while triaging sales and non-sales inquiries, causing slow response times and delayed onboarding.
Architected Solution:
  • Active Logic: Recognizes Enterprise Signals, prompts for meetings vs. answering
  • Compliance Guardrails: Enforces legal and security protocols, prevent guessing in high stakes settings
  • Strategic Research: Drove best practices for context driving research before response
Impact Reduced manager dependency for edge case scenarios, improved Time to Action for new hires, drove higher-quality meetings while reducing time spent on non-sales inquiries
