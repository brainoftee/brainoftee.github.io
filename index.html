Strategic Initiatives

Key Projects & Solutions

01

Sales Development Territory Split

The Challenge: 70% of inbound leads from "Greenfield" accounts forced Territory ADRs to spend majority time on low-probability volume rather than strategic accounts.
The Solution:
  • Created specialized Inbound ADR team for high-volume unowned leads
  • Elevated Corporate ADRs to focus exclusively on named accounts
  • Enabled deeper territory alignment with AEs
Impact Optimized workflow by aligning resources with lead sources, delivering higher-quality opportunities to AEs
02

Performance-Based Advancement

The Challenge: Traditional tenure-based progression led to ramp inefficiencies when ADRs promoted to AE roles without skill mastery.
The Solution:
  • Phase 1: Product Knowledge & Tech Landscape mastery
  • Phase 2: MEDDPICC & consultative selling frameworks
  • Phase 3: Advanced negotiation & account expansion
Impact Decoupled "time in seat" from "readiness," ensuring promotions based on demonstrated competency
03

Territory & Packaging Strategy Shift

The Context: FY26 brought dramatic territory shift to SMB segment (<250 employees) with new "SSO Lite" threatening to cannibalize Enterprise revenue.
Key Insights:
  • FinTech: Positioned Enterprise as "Audit Readiness" requirement for PCI-DSS/SOC 2
  • Healthcare: Leveraged BAA support gap to disqualify Team plan for PHI handling
  • SaaS/Tech: Sold Private API Network as "Internal App Store" solving sprawl chaos
Impact Shifted from selling "seats" to selling "Governance Infrastructure," targeting architectural inflection points
04

Messaging Performance Optimization

The Insight: Analysis of Gong Flows debunked "more volume = more results" assumption.
Data-Driven Changes:
  • "LinkedIn - Job Change" flow achieved 54% open rate, 11% click rate
  • Manual personalized emails outperformed automated steps for meetings
  • Implemented Trigger-Based Flows over generic vertical messaging
Impact Mandated "80% effort above Power Line" (Directors+) with high-effort personalization on high-value personas
05

Trigger-Based Selling Operations

The Challenge: 1,169 alerts over 30 days showed 37.55% were low-intent noise while high-value signals received generic follow-ups.
Signal-to-Play Matrix:
  • Fear & Limitation: Usage threshold alerts → "risk of blocked automation" narrative
  • Consolidation: Integration signals → "Platform consolidation" pitch to DevOps
  • Governance: Trust Center views → "Admin time savings" to IT leads
Impact Deprecated dead signals, reduced rep fatigue, shifted License Expansion to target Senior Leadership
06

Custom AI Support GPT

The Challenge: ADR team faced knowledge fragmentation across disconnected resources, causing slow response times and inconsistent Salesforce hygiene.
Architected Solution:
  • Active Logic: Recognizes Enterprise Signals, prompts for meetings vs. answering
  • Compliance Guardrails: Enforces MNDA/DPA protocols, prevents legal guessing
  • Strategic Routing: Mandates Looker checks for data-backed responses
Impact Reduced manager dependency, improved Time to Action for new hires, drove higher-quality meetings (POM scores)
07

ADR Onboarding Machine

The Goal: Eliminate ad-hoc shadowing while protecting active pipeline from unprepared reps.
4-Week Gated Progression:
  • Week 1-2: "Postman Pal" mentorship for culture integration
  • Week 3: Sandbox on recycled leads for low-risk skill development
  • Week 4: 30-minute Certification Defense proving data literacy before live lead routing
Impact Structured "safe failure" curriculum reduced ramp time while maintaining pipeline quality
08

Vertical Enablement: FinServ Deep Dive

The Shift: Moved beyond generic pitching to mastering FinServ-specific vocabulary and pain points.
Training Framework:
  • Market Drivers: Banking-as-a-Service, Open Banking (PSD2), 20% revenue from APIs
  • Compliance Wedge: Positioned as solution to microservices governance using Citi case study
  • Case Studies: Axis Bank onboarding reduction from 10 days to 2 days
Impact Reps learned to pitch APIs as board-level revenue strategy, not IT plumbing
