Professional Overview

I've spent 10+ years in GTM operations and enablement roles solving a specific problem: how to turn high-performing teams into predictable engines. I've done this work across EdTech and DevOps platforms, and the pattern I've seen is consistent: most revenue challenges aren't talent issues, they're structural ones. When information is scattered and drifts from customer language, CSAT and NRR stagnate. When enablement focuses on product features instead of business outcomes, deals stall in discovery. When strategy is built on assumptions instead of data, growth becomes reactive instead of repeatable.

My approach starts with diagnosis. I use data to identify where friction exists, then I build the systems that eliminate it. That means designing new territory splits, creating competency-based training programs, or developing vertical-specific playbooks that help reps speak the language of the buyer. I thrive in roles that demand planning, analysis, and execution under pressure because that's where strategy proves itself. What I've consistently found is that when you align structure with intent (when people, process, and data work together) growth becomes a system.
