## Visual Style

### Overall Aesthetic

- **Warm minimalism** with soft, muted tones
- **Clean and airy** with generous white space
- **Sophisticated feminine** palette (not overly girly)
- **Professional with personality**
- **Modern glassmorphism** effects (subtle blur, transparency)

### Gradients

**Primary Gradient (Warm):**
`linear-gradient(135deg, #F7E1D7 0%, #EDAFB8 100%)`

**Accent Gradient:**
`linear-gradient(135deg, #E89AAA, #D68B9E)`

**Text Gradient:**
`linear-gradient(135deg, #C4748A, #A8616F)`

### Shadows

- Soft, warm-tinted shadows using rose/mauve undertones
- Shadow color base: `rgba(196, 116, 138, opacity)`
- Opacity range: 8% (subtle) to 20% (prominent)
- Always soft and diffused, never harsh

### Border Radius

- Cards/Containers: 12-20px (rounded, not sharp)
- Buttons: Fully rounded (pill shape) or 12px
- Images: 12-20px rounded corners
- Tags/Badges: Fully rounded (pill shape)

## Imagery Guidelines

### Photography Style

- **Warm, natural lighting** (golden hour preferred)
- **Soft focus backgrounds** with sharp subjects
- **Neutral or warm color grading**
- **Professional but approachable** poses
- **Clean, uncluttered compositions**

### Illustration Style

- **Minimalist line art** or **soft gradient illustrations**
- **Warm color palette** matching brand colors
- **Geometric shapes** with rounded corners
- **Abstract data visualizations** in brand colors
- **Icons:** Simple, outlined or filled, consistent stroke width

### Infographic Style

- **Clean, structured layouts** with clear hierarchy
- **Generous white space** between elements
- **Accent color for key data points**
- **Soft backgrounds** (Powder Petal or Dust Grey)
- **Rose Mauve** for charts, graphs, and callout numbers
- **Rounded corners** on all elements
- **Subtle shadows** for depth

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's
- Use warm, muted tones
- Maintain generous white space
- Use rounded corners consistently
- Apply soft, warm-tinted shadows
- Keep designs clean and uncluttered
- Use the accent color (Rose Mauve) sparingly for impact
- Maintain professional sophistication

### Don'ts
- Avoid harsh, saturated colors
- Don't use sharp corners or harsh edges
- Avoid cluttered, busy layouts
- Don't use cold blue/grey shadows
- Avoid overly feminine or "cute" styling
- Don't overuse the accent color
- Avoid harsh black (#000000) - use Iron Grey instead
