Personal Overview

Raised in Dallas, but deeply rooted in Austin, Texas for the last 10 years. I moved to Austin for the tech startup scene and stayed for the BBQ! Just kidding, I stayed for the amazing community of driven and kind folks that tend to gather around these parts. The BBQ is definitely an added plus.

My mornings typically start at the gym, and my evenings are often spent diving into sci-fi world-building, self-taught coding projects, or hosting community events geared towards connection. Bringing intentionality to all parts of my life is important to me. I'm a dedicated dog mom to a sweet tripod pup named Ken, a senior cat named Shadow, and a proud homeowner of a fixer upper in the heart of North Austin. I make frequent trips up to Dallas (pup and kitty in tow!) to spend time with my family, as well as squeeze in trips around the states and globally when I can. Most recently, I went on a 10-day yoga retreat in Morocco, so ask me for tips if you ever want to go!

I'm a major "planner" in life, whether it's travel, events, or my day-to-day, I always have a spreadsheet and a folder of resources spun up for ideating. That said, spontanaeity is straight up my alley, plans are great but going with the chaotic flow of life is what brings the spice. (Like the time I signed up for a Spartan race 2 months out with zero training...)

Feel free to reach out to work together on any projects related to community support through education or fundraising, hosting events, or other creative ventures.

A Breathe Between Us

Science Fiction • Novel WIP

The first thing that came back to Anastazya was her mother’s laugh.

Not the details of her voice, but the way her laughter would fill up a room. Sometimes an airy giggle, playful and coy, you had to pay attention to catch it. Eventually erupting into a full-bodied cackle, incapable of holding back the expression of joy.

It felt foreign at first, but the longer she lingered, the more it solidified. Pixelated images flooded in tethered to her mother's laugh.

Streaks of light danced into fragments of a larger image that flashed briefly: a young boy’s mischievous smile, an older man’s weathered hands, the scent of rain on a humid summer’s day, blinding sunlight bouncing off a cement driveway littered with children’s toys and half finished woodworking projects.

Is this death? She wondered. The fragments steadied into streams of images, tied to scents, sounds, and feelings. This wasn’t dying, this was remembering.

Not just any memories, her own memories. Her family?

A Reflective Essay

To ask "why" is so deeply human, so clearly awake and conscious. Seeking truth, seeking answers. These take our day to day lives and move away fro reactivity and survival into higher thought patterns so deeply embedded into us and ask to go higher, demand to go higher.

Every soul is one of an adventurer. We chose to enter this life on a mission to discover: What does it mean to be human? Our sense are our gifts - to taste the sweetness of fruit, the smell of grass after a heavy rain, to lock eyes with our lovers, to hear our names called across a room, to feel the warm of the sun. Our bodies are gifted with emotions - heart swelling joy, bellyaching laughter, and still the necessary paralyzing pain of grief, the volcanic pressure of anger. All are necessary in the heroic journey we take towards finding our purpose.

Expansion requires growth which requires pain. If we lives our lives with no pain we would rob ourselves of it means to be human. You are here! You are alive! You are human. Embrace it. Love it. Allow it. Resistance to being imperfect will not serve you, my love. There is nothing perfect in this world, that's exactly what makes it perfect. You are a reflection of an imperfect world and your life is a reflection of you.

We continue to evolve and now we trek into the unknown as pioneers of our destiny. Now, to evolve further we must go against our nature. There are no lions or bears now, but there is social anxiety, there are career step backs, there are relationships we cannot see ourselves living without. These are our new bears. Instead of running from the bear, we must turn around and face it. That is where our next evolutionary growth spurt lives. Can you turn towards your flaws? Can you show them as much love and grace as you do to your favorite parts of you?

We constantly are building the path of our adventures, laying down the tracks as we go. Do you choose to go up the hill of resistance and self criticism or down the slope of acceptance and allowance? To go deeper down this path of allowing flaws requires that we retrain our subconscious and address our inner critics. In this spirit of allowing flaws, do not expect yourself to perfect this process now.

We must first become aware and practice, overtime it will become easier.
